# v0.9.0 local trial checklist

## Scope

Shared pond/soil/air water; bounded moss expansion and surface colonization; fungal fruit/rest cycles and finite daughter clusters; reversible visible drought/heat/waterlogging stress; dark weathering and soft mold on wood. Preserve warm artwork, existing authored layouts and permanent plant survival. No publication or upload is part of development.

## Required checks

- Strict types and full core/desktop/renderer tests; new-field validation and exact pre-v0.9 backups fail closed.
- Multi-year normal, dry, hot, wet, source-free and pond-fed simulations; finite unique offspring; closed water conservation and open loss; authored objects never destroyed; recovery after care.
- Compare long catch-up with smaller time steps; record tolerances rather than assert scientific precision.
- Inspect real renderer normal/dry/wet/long-term scenes, including house/stairs coverage, fern/Fittonia wilt, wood mold and fungal phases; no background change or geometry holes.
- Isolated Electron IPC/save/restart, existing gestures, and packaged candidate smoke checks. Never use the real user's save for tests.
- Prepare local Windows ZIP and allowlisted source archive. Record hashes and actual exclusions; no signing/macOS/publication claim without evidence.

## Compatibility

Schema stays 2 with strict optional extensions: `Plant.ecology`, `Decoration.colonization`, `ObjectCondition.mold`, `Pond.exchange`. Older v0.8 readers reject these fields. Available compatible original primary and backup bytes are exclusively preserved in `terrarium.before-v0.9-primary.json` and `terrarium.before-v0.9-backup.json` before rotation; failure blocks overwrite. Earlier gates must never label v0.9 data as an old-version recovery copy.

## Verification record

The local delivery includes `Mosslight-0.9.0-VERIFICATION.md` beside the archives with actual evidence and hashes. This checklist is not a claim that those checks have passed. See [Ecology](ECOLOGY.md) for science-inspired mechanisms and remaining approximation limits.
